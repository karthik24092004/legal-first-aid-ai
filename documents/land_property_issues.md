Land and Property Issues

Incident Summary

A person is facing a land, rental, housing, or property-related dispute such as security deposit not being returned, illegal eviction, rent disagreement, property damage, or refusal to pay for repairs.

Security Deposit Not Returned

Immediate Steps

1. Review the rental agreement or written understanding.
2. Collect proof of deposit payment.
3. Request return of deposit in writing.
4. Ask for written reasons if deductions are made.
5. Preserve all rent and deposit payment records.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Rental agreement.
2. Deposit payment receipt or bank transaction proof.
3. Rent payment records.
4. Messages, emails, or notices exchanged with landlord.
5. Photos or videos of property condition at move-out.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station if threats, force, or illegal lockout is involved.
2. Rent Control Authority or local housing authority if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer for civil recovery.

Important Notes

1. Keep communication in writing.
2. Do not threaten or use force.
3. Preserve proof of property handover.
4. Maintain copies of all notices and complaints.

Illegal Eviction

Immediate Steps

1. Preserve any eviction notice or message.
2. Do not vacate immediately without understanding your rights.
3. Record details of lockout, threats, or forced removal.
4. Contact local authorities if force or intimidation is used.
5. Seek legal help if eviction is done without proper process.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Rental agreement.
2. Rent receipts.
3. Eviction notice or messages.
4. Photos or videos of lockout or property access denial.
5. Witness details if available.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station if force, threats, or illegal lockout occurs.
2. Rent Control Authority or local housing authority if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer.

Important Notes

1. Avoid physical confrontation.
2. Keep copies of all rental and payment documents.
3. Record dates and times of eviction-related incidents.
4. Preserve all communication with landlord or property owner.

Rent Dispute

Immediate Steps

1. Review the rental agreement.
2. Compare disputed rent amount with written terms.
3. Communicate the dispute in writing.
4. Preserve rent payment records.
5. Avoid stopping communication abruptly.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Rental agreement.
2. Rent receipts or bank transfer records.
3. Messages or emails about rent demands.
4. Any notices received.
5. Maintenance or utility bills if relevant.

Authorities To Contact

1. Rent Control Authority or local housing authority if applicable.
2. Legal aid service or lawyer.
3. Local Police Station only if threats, violence, or harassment occurs.

Important Notes

1. Keep all discussions documented.
2. Avoid verbal-only agreements.
3. Maintain proof of payments.
4. Preserve notices and written demands.

Property Damage

Immediate Steps

1. Document the damage immediately.
2. Take photos and videos from multiple angles.
3. Identify the person or party responsible if possible.
4. Request repair or compensation in writing.
5. File a complaint if damage was intentional or serious.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Photos and videos of damage.
2. Repair estimates or bills.
3. Witness details.
4. Messages or admissions by the responsible party.
5. CCTV footage if available.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station if damage was intentional, serious, or accompanied by threats.
2. Property association or management office if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer for compensation claims.

Important Notes

1. Do not repair major damage before documenting it.
2. Preserve bills and repair estimates.
3. Keep communication in writing.
4. Maintain copies of complaints and reference numbers.
