UPI Fraud

Incident Summary:
Money has been lost through unauthorized UPI transactions, phishing links, fake payment requests, QR code scams, or account compromise.

Immediate Steps:
1. Contact your bank immediately.
2. Freeze or block affected accounts if possible.
3. Change banking and UPI passwords.
4. Report the incident to the National Cyber Crime Portal.
5. Call cybercrime helpline 1930 as soon as possible.

Evidence To Preserve:
1. Transaction IDs.
2. Screenshots of messages.
3. Fraudulent payment requests.
4. Bank statements.
5. Phone numbers and account details involved.
6. Email communications related to the fraud.

Authorities To Contact:
1. Bank Customer Care.
2. Cyber Crime Cell.
3. National Cyber Crime Portal.

Important Notes:
1. Faster reporting increases chances of fund recovery.
2. Never share OTPs or UPI PINs.
3. Beware of fake customer support numbers.
4. Preserve all communication records.

Fraud Reporting Timeline

Report the fraud immediately.
Reporting within the earliest possible time improves the chances of freezing suspicious transactions and recovering funds.

Wrong UPI Transfer

Incident Summary

Money has been accidentally sent to the wrong UPI ID, wrong mobile number, or unintended recipient.

Immediate Steps

1. Contact your bank immediately.
2. Raise a wrong transaction complaint with the bank or payment app.
3. Note the UPI transaction ID, date, time, amount, and recipient details.
4. Request the bank or payment app to assist with reversal if possible.
5. Do not contact unknown recipients aggressively or threaten them.

Evidence To Preserve

1. UPI transaction ID.
2. Screenshot of payment confirmation.
3. Bank statement showing debit.
4. Recipient UPI ID or mobile number.
5. Complaint reference number from bank or payment app.

Authorities To Contact

1. Bank Customer Care.
2. Payment App Support.
3. Cyber Crime Cell only if fraud, cheating, or refusal after deception is involved.

Important Notes

1. Wrong transfers may not always be reversible automatically.
2. Faster reporting improves chances of assistance.
3. Preserve all complaint reference numbers.
4. Keep all communication polite and documented.
