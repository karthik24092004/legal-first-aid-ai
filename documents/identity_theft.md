Identity Theft

Incident Summary

Personal information, documents, credentials, or identity details have been used without authorization.

Immediate Steps

1. Identify which personal information has been compromised.
2. Change passwords and security credentials.
3. Inform relevant institutions if accounts are affected.
4. Monitor financial and online accounts.
5. File complaints if unauthorized use is detected.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Screenshots of fraudulent profiles or activity.
2. Copies of affected documents.
3. Emails, messages, and notifications.
4. Transaction records if financial misuse occurred.
5. Complaint acknowledgment receipts.

Authorities To Contact

1. Cyber Crime Cell.
2. Local Police Station.
3. Relevant issuing authorities.

Important Notes

1. Monitor accounts regularly for suspicious activity.
2. Preserve evidence of misuse.
3. Record complaint and reference numbers.
4. Inform affected institutions promptly.
