Account Hacking

Incident Summary

An online account such as email, social media, messaging, or other digital services has been accessed without authorization.

Immediate Steps

1. Attempt password reset immediately.
2. Enable two-factor authentication if available.
3. Log out of all active sessions.
4. Review recent account activity.
5. Notify contacts if the account has been misused.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Screenshots of unauthorized activity.
2. Login alerts and notifications.
3. Emails received from the service provider.
4. Messages sent from the compromised account.
5. Account recovery records.

Authorities To Contact

1. Service Provider Support Team.
2. Cyber Crime Cell.
3. Local Police Station if financial or personal harm has occurred.

Important Notes

1. Use strong and unique passwords.
2. Preserve all recovery and support communications.
3. Monitor linked accounts for suspicious activity.
4. Record complaint and reference numbers.
