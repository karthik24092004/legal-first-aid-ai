Evidence Collection

General Principles:
1. Preserve original documents whenever possible.
2. Do not edit screenshots or recordings.
3. Keep backups of all digital evidence.
4. Record dates and times accurately.

Useful Evidence:
1. CCTV footage.
2. Photographs.
3. Videos.
4. Emails.
5. Chat messages.
6. Transaction receipts.
7. Witness statements.
8. Audio recordings where legally permissible.

Important Notes:
1. Evidence should be collected as soon as possible.
2. Multiple copies should be maintained.
3. Store digital evidence securely.
