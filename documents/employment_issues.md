Employment Issues

Incident Summary

An employee or job applicant is facing employment-related problems such as unpaid salary, delayed final settlement, wrongful termination, experience letter withholding, offer letter disputes, or workplace-related documentation issues.

Unpaid Salary

Immediate Steps

1. Review employment agreement, offer letter, or salary terms.
2. Verify salary payment records and bank statements.
3. Contact the employer or payroll department in writing.
4. Request clarification regarding the delay.
5. Preserve all employment and payment records.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Offer letter or employment contract.
2. Salary slips.
3. Bank statements showing salary history.
4. Emails, messages, and payroll communications.
5. Attendance records if available.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department.
2. Labour Department or Labour Commissioner if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer if the dispute continues.

Important Notes

1. Keep communication in writing.
2. Preserve all salary-related records.
3. Maintain copies of complaints and responses.
4. Record complaint reference numbers.

Final Settlement Delayed

Immediate Steps

1. Review company policies and exit documentation.
2. Contact the employer or Human Resources Department.
3. Request a written update regarding settlement status.
4. Preserve resignation and exit-related records.
5. Follow up through official channels.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Resignation letter.
2. Exit clearance documents.
3. Final settlement communications.
4. Salary and payment records.
5. Emails and messages with employer.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department.
2. Labour Department or Labour Commissioner if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer.

Important Notes

1. Preserve all exit-related documentation.
2. Keep records of follow-up communications.
3. Maintain copies of settlement requests and responses.

Experience Letter Withheld

Immediate Steps

1. Request the experience letter in writing.
2. Preserve all communications with the employer.
3. Follow the company's grievance process if available.
4. Keep copies of resignation and clearance documents.
5. Escalate through appropriate channels if necessary.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Employment contract or offer letter.
2. Resignation acceptance.
3. Exit clearance documents.
4. Emails and messages requesting the experience letter.
5. Company responses.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department.
2. Labour Department or Labour Commissioner if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer.

Important Notes

1. Maintain copies of all employment records.
2. Keep communication professional and documented.
3. Preserve responses received from the employer.

Wrongful Termination

Immediate Steps

1. Review the termination notice and employment agreement.
2. Request written reasons for termination if not provided.
3. Preserve all employment-related records.
4. Avoid deleting emails, messages, or workplace communications.
5. Seek professional legal guidance if necessary.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Termination letter or notice.
2. Employment contract.
3. Performance reviews if available.
4. Emails and workplace communications.
5. Witness information if relevant.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department.
2. Labour Department or Labour Commissioner if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer.

Important Notes

1. Preserve all termination-related records.
2. Maintain copies of complaints and responses.
3. Record dates and details of termination-related discussions.

Offer Letter Disputes

Immediate Steps

1. Review the offer letter carefully.
2. Compare promised terms with written terms.
3. Preserve recruitment communications.
4. Request clarification in writing.
5. Avoid relying on verbal assurances alone.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Offer letter.
2. Recruitment emails and messages.
3. Job advertisements.
4. Interview communications.
5. Written representations made by the employer.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department.
2. Labour Department if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer.

Important Notes

1. Preserve all recruitment records.
2. Keep communication in writing.
3. Maintain copies of complaints and responses.

Internship Stipend Not Paid

Immediate Steps

1. Review the internship offer letter, agreement, or written stipend terms.
2. Check whether stipend amount and payment timeline were mentioned in writing.
3. Contact the employer or internship coordinator in writing.
4. Request clarification regarding delayed or unpaid stipend.
5. Preserve all internship-related records.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Internship offer letter or agreement.
2. Emails or messages mentioning stipend.
3. Attendance records or work submissions.
4. Bank statements showing non-payment.
5. Communications with employer or internship coordinator.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department or internship coordinator.
2. Labour Department or Labour Commissioner if applicable.
3. Legal aid service or lawyer if the dispute continues.

Important Notes

1. Keep communication professional and written.
2. Preserve proof of work completed.
3. Maintain copies of stipend-related promises and responses.
4. Record complaint and reference numbers if a complaint is filed.
