Bank Fraud

Incident Summary

Unauthorized transactions, account compromise, suspicious withdrawals, or fraudulent banking activity have occurred.

Immediate Steps

1. Contact the bank immediately.
2. Request account freeze or transaction blocking if necessary.
3. Change banking passwords and security credentials.
4. Report the incident through official fraud reporting channels.
5. Review recent account activity.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Transaction IDs.
2. Bank statements.
3. SMS and email alerts.
4. Screenshots of suspicious activity.
5. Account details involved in disputed transactions.

Authorities To Contact

1. Bank Customer Care.
2. Cyber Crime Cell.
3. National Cyber Crime Portal.

Important Notes

1. Faster reporting improves the chances of preventing further losses.
2. Never share OTPs or banking credentials.
3. Preserve all communication with the bank.
4. Record complaint and reference numbers.
