Bike Theft

Incident Summary:
A bike or two-wheeler has been stolen from a public or private location.

Immediate Steps:
1. Confirm that the vehicle has actually been stolen and was not moved by family, friends, security personnel, or authorities.
2. Note the exact location, date, and time when the vehicle was last seen.
3. Contact the nearest police station immediately.
4. File a First Information Report (FIR) and obtain a copy or reference number.
5. Inform local security personnel, apartment management, college authorities, or workplace security if the theft occurred on their premises.

Evidence To Preserve:
1. Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC).
2. Purchase invoice or ownership proof.
3. Photographs of the vehicle.
4. Insurance policy documents.
5. CCTV footage if available.
6. Parking receipts or entry logs.
7. Witness contact information.

Authorities To Contact:
1. Local Police Station.
2. Traffic or Transport Department if required.
3. Property management or institution security office.

Insurance Claim After Bike Theft

1. File an FIR at the nearest police station.
2. Inform the insurance company as soon as possible.
3. Submit FIR copy, insurance policy details, vehicle registration certificate, and proof of ownership.
4. Keep copies of all submitted documents.
5. Ask the insurance company for claim reference number.

Authorities / Organizations To Contact

1. Local Police Station.
2. Insurance Company.
3. Transport Department if required.
4. Property management or institution security office if theft happened in a private campus, apartment, office, or college.

Important Notes:
1. Record the FIR number carefully.
2. Maintain copies of all complaints and communications.
3. Check online marketplaces for suspicious listings.
4. Follow up periodically with investigating officers.
