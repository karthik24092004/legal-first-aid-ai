Extortion

Incident Summary

A person is being threatened, intimidated, or coerced into providing money, property, services, or other benefits.

Immediate Steps

1. Do not comply with demands.
2. Preserve all communications and evidence.
3. Avoid direct confrontation if personal safety is at risk.
4. Inform trusted family members or friends.
5. Seek immediate assistance if threats involve violence.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Screenshots of messages.
2. Call recordings if legally available.
3. Payment requests and account details.
4. Dates and times of communications.
5. Witness information if available.
6. UPI ID, payment request, QR code, or account details shared by the offender.
7. Username, profile link, phone number, or account details of the offender.
8. Dates and times of threats and payment demands.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station.
2. Cyber Crime Cell if online communication is involved.

Important Notes

1. Do not send money.
2. Preserve evidence before blocking accounts.
3. Maintain copies of all complaints and communications.
4. Record complaint and reference numbers.
