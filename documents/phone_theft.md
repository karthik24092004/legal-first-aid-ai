Phone Theft

Incident Summary

A mobile phone has been stolen, lost under suspicious circumstances, or is believed to be in unauthorized possession.

Immediate Steps

1. Attempt to locate the device using available tracking services.
2. Lock the device remotely if possible.
3. Contact the mobile service provider and request SIM blocking.
4. Change passwords for important accounts linked to the device.
5. File a police complaint if theft is suspected.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Purchase invoice or proof of ownership.
2. IMEI number of the device.
3. Screenshots of device location if available.
4. Call records and account activity.
5. Details of the last known location.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station.
2. Mobile Service Provider.
3. Cyber Crime Cell if unauthorized account access occurs.

Important Notes

1. Block the SIM card immediately to prevent misuse.
2. Record the complaint reference number.
3. Monitor banking and social media accounts for suspicious activity.
4. Keep copies of all complaints and communications.
