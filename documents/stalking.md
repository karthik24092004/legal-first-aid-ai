Stalking

Incident Summary

Repeated following, monitoring, unwanted contact, surveillance, or harassment that causes fear, distress, or concern for personal safety.

Immediate Steps

1. Avoid direct confrontation if personal safety is at risk.
2. Inform trusted family members or friends.
3. Change daily routines if necessary.
4. Seek immediate assistance if threats are involved.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Screenshots of messages.
2. Call logs.
3. Photos or videos.
4. Dates, times, and locations of incidents.
5. Witness statements if available.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station.
2. Women's Help Desk if applicable.
3. Cyber Crime Cell if online stalking is involved.

Important Notes

1. Do not delete evidence.
2. Block accounts only after preserving evidence.
3. Maintain a written record of incidents.
