Consumer Disputes

Incident Summary

A consumer is facing issues related to goods or services such as non-delivery of products, defective items, refund disputes, warranty disputes, misleading advertisements, billing errors, or poor service.

Product Not Delivered

Immediate Steps

1. Verify the order status and delivery timeline.
2. Contact the seller or service provider through official channels.
3. Request a written update regarding delivery.
4. Preserve all order and payment records.
5. Escalate the complaint if no response is received.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Order confirmation.
2. Payment receipts or transaction records.
3. Screenshots of product listings.
4. Messages, emails, and chat records with the seller.
5. Tracking details and delivery status records.

Authorities To Contact

1. Consumer Helpline or Consumer Protection Authority if applicable.
2. Online marketplace customer support if the purchase was made through a platform.
3. Local Police Station if fraud or deliberate deception is suspected.

Important Notes

1. Keep all communication in writing.
2. Do not delete order or payment records.
3. Maintain copies of complaints and support tickets.
4. Record complaint reference numbers.

Defective Product

Immediate Steps

1. Document the defect immediately.
2. Contact the seller or manufacturer.
3. Request replacement, repair, or refund as applicable.
4. Preserve packaging and invoices.
5. Follow warranty procedures if available.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Photos and videos of defects.
2. Purchase invoice.
3. Warranty documents.
4. Product packaging.
5. Communication records with seller or manufacturer.

Authorities To Contact

1. Seller or manufacturer support team.
2. Consumer Protection Authority if applicable.
3. Online marketplace support if purchased through a platform.

Important Notes

1. Do not discard defective products before documenting the issue.
2. Preserve warranty and purchase records.
3. Maintain copies of complaints and responses.

Refund Not Received

Immediate Steps

1. Review refund policy and promised timelines.
2. Contact the seller or service provider.
3. Request written confirmation of refund status.
4. Escalate the complaint if delays continue.
5. Preserve payment and communication records.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Payment receipts.
2. Refund requests.
3. Emails and messages regarding refund status.
4. Order details.
5. Complaint reference numbers.

Authorities To Contact

1. Consumer Protection Authority if applicable.
2. Online marketplace support.
3. Payment service provider if relevant.

Important Notes

1. Keep records of all refund-related communication.
2. Preserve payment proofs and complaint references.
3. Follow escalation procedures before pursuing legal remedies.

Warranty Disputes

Immediate Steps

1. Review warranty terms and conditions.
2. Contact the seller or manufacturer.
3. Request written reasons if warranty service is denied.
4. Preserve warranty documents and invoices.
5. Escalate unresolved disputes through consumer protection channels.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Warranty card or warranty certificate.
2. Purchase invoice.
3. Product service records.
4. Written denial of warranty claims.
5. Communication records.

Authorities To Contact

1. Manufacturer support team.
2. Seller support team.
3. Consumer Protection Authority if applicable.

Important Notes

1. Preserve all warranty-related documents.
2. Keep communication in writing.
3. Record complaint and service request numbers.
