Filing a Complaint

Steps:
1. Gather all available evidence.
2. Prepare a written description of the incident.
3. Note dates, times, and locations.
4. Visit the appropriate authority.
5. Obtain acknowledgement or reference number.
6. Keep copies of submitted documents.

Important Notes:
1. Follow up periodically.
2. Maintain records of communications.
3. Escalate to higher authorities if no action is taken.
