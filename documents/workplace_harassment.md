Workplace Harassment

Incident Summary

An employee is experiencing unwanted behavior, intimidation, discrimination, bullying, verbal abuse, or harassment within the workplace.

Immediate Steps

1. Document all incidents as accurately as possible.
2. Preserve emails, messages, and related communications.
3. Inform a supervisor, manager, or designated authority.
4. Follow the organization's grievance procedure.
5. Seek support from trusted colleagues if necessary.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Emails and messages.
2. Meeting records and notes.
3. Witness information.
4. Screenshots of communications.
5. Complaint submissions and acknowledgments.

Authorities To Contact

1. Human Resources Department.
2. Internal Complaints Committee if applicable.
3. Local Police Station if criminal threats or violence occur.

Important Notes

1. Maintain a written record of incidents.
2. Preserve evidence before filing complaints.
3. Keep copies of all submissions and responses.
4. Avoid deleting relevant communications.
