Blackmail

Incident Summary

A person is threatening, coercing, or intimidating another person to obtain money, personal information, images, videos, or other benefits.

Immediate Steps

1. Do not comply with demands.
2. Preserve all communications.
3. Avoid deleting messages.
4. Seek immediate assistance if threats involve personal safety.
5. Inform trusted family members or friends.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Screenshots of messages.
2. Social media usernames and profile links.
3. Photos, videos, or files involved.
4. Dates and times of communications.
5. Payment requests or account details shared by the offender.
6. UPI ID, payment request, QR code, or account details shared by the offender.
7. Username, profile link, phone number, or account details of the offender.
8. Dates and times of threats and payment demands.

Authorities To Contact

1. Local Police Station.
2. Cyber Crime Cell.
3. National Cyber Crime Portal.

Important Notes

1. Do not send money.
2. Preserve evidence before blocking accounts.
3. Report the account on the platform after preserving evidence.
4. Maintain copies of all complaints and reference numbers.
5. Keep records of all communications.
