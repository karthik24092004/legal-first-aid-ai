Job Scam

Incident Summary

A person has received a suspicious job offer, recruitment request, work-from-home opportunity, or employment proposal involving fraudulent activity.

Immediate Steps

1. Stop further communication if fraud is suspected.
2. Verify the employer through official sources.
3. Avoid making payments for registration, training, or processing fees.
4. Preserve all communication records.
5. Report suspicious activity to relevant authorities.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Emails and messages.
2. Offer letters and attachments.
3. Payment receipts if money was transferred.
4. Contact details of recruiters.
5. Screenshots of advertisements and job postings.

Authorities To Contact

1. Cyber Crime Cell.
2. National Cyber Crime Portal.
3. Local Police Station if financial loss has occurred.

Important Notes

1. Genuine employers rarely demand upfront payments.
2. Verify company information independently.
3. Preserve all evidence of communication.
4. Record complaint and reference numbers.
