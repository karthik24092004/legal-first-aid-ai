Cyberbullying

Incident Summary

A person is experiencing repeated online harassment, abusive messages, threats, humiliation, impersonation, or targeted attacks through digital platforms.

Immediate Steps

1. Avoid responding to abusive messages.
2. Preserve evidence before blocking accounts.
3. Inform trusted family members, friends, or guardians.
4. Report abusive content to the platform.
5. Seek immediate assistance if threats involve personal safety.

Evidence To Preserve

1. Screenshots of messages and posts.
2. Usernames and profile links.
3. Dates and times of incidents.
4. Emails and notifications related to harassment.
5. Witness statements if available.

Authorities To Contact

1. Cyber Crime Cell.
2. Local Police Station if threats are involved.
3. Platform Support Team.

Important Notes

1. Do not delete evidence.
2. Preserve evidence before reporting or blocking accounts.
3. Maintain records of all incidents.
4. Keep copies of complaints and reference numbers.
